# Saturday Brunch Cook-Along: A Lazy Chinese-Style 手抓饼 Edition

Welcome back to the Saturday cook-along! This week we're going full lazy-brunch mode with a Chinese street-food classic: the **手抓饼 (shǒu zhuā bǐng)** — a golden, flaky, hand-held pancake wrapped around egg, lettuce and ham, with a bowl of soup and a milk tea alongside. Everything below is pulled from the [HowToCook](https://github.com/Anduin2017/HowToCook) collection (程序员做饭指南), so you know it's been kitchen-tested by actual programmers.

## The Star: 手抓饼 (Hand-Held Pancake)

- **Difficulty:** ★★ (beginner-friendly)
- **Serves:** 1 (one batch feeds 1–2)
- **Time:** about 40 minutes from mixing to table
- **Estimated calories:** ~1050 kcal per serving

手抓饼 is crisp outside, soft and chewy inside, with distinct flaky layers. The dough is a half-hot-water dough (半烫面)， which keeps it tender and pliable, and it's wrapped around a fried egg, lettuce, ham and a slice of cheese for a balanced, grab-and-go brunch.

### Ingredients (per serving)

- 面粉 (all-purpose flour) — 200 克
- 开水 (boiling water) — 100 毫升
- 冷水 (cold water) — 50 毫升
- 食用油 (cooking oil) — 15 毫升
- 盐 (salt) — 3 克
- 鸡蛋 (egg) — 1 个
- 生菜 (lettuce) — 30 克
- 火腿 (ham) — 30 克
- 芝士片 (cheese slice) — 1 片

### Steps

1. Put the flour in a bowl, pour in the boiling water and stir until it forms flakes, then add the cold water and knead into a smooth dough. Cover with a damp cloth and rest for 20 minutes.
2. Divide the dough into ~100 克 portions, round them, and roll each into a thin sheet.
3. Brush the surface evenly with oil, sprinkle with salt, roll it up into a snail shape, and let it relax for 10 minutes.
4. Roll the dough out again into an evenly thin pancake.
5. Heat oil in a pan and pan-fry over low heat until both sides are golden and blistered.
6. Layer a fried egg, lettuce, ham and the cheese slice onto the hot pancake, roll it up, and it's ready.

### Lazy-brunch notes

- Mixing boiling + cold water for the dough is what gives the wrapper its chew and flexibility.
- Don't shortcut the 20-minute rest — the dough won't roll out thin enough without it.
- Keep total fillings around 100 克 or under so it stays hand-holdable.
- Spare raw wrappers keep 24 hours in the fridge; bring back to room temp and re-roll when you want round two tomorrow.

## On the Side: 奶油蘑菇汤 (Creamy Mushroom Soup)

- **Difficulty:** ★ (the easiest soup in the book)
- **Time:** about 30 minutes
- **Estimated calories:** ~426 kcal per serving

A silky French-style cream soup — mushroom aroma meets milk. It takes nearly zero skill and makes the pancake brunch feel like a proper café spread.

### Ingredients (per serving)

- 白蘑菇 (white mushrooms) — 200 克
- 洋葱 (onion) — 50 克
- 黄油 (butter) — 15 克
- 面粉 (flour) — 10 克
- 牛奶 (milk) — 200 毫升
- 淡奶油 (heavy cream) — 30 毫升
- 清水 (water) — 100 毫升
- 盐 (salt) — 2 克
- 黑胡椒碎 (cracked black pepper) — 1 克

### Steps (quick version)

1. Slice the mushrooms, dice the onion.
2. Melt the butter over low heat and sweat the onion until translucent.
3. Add the mushrooms and cook until softened, then stir in the flour.
4. Pour in the milk and water, bring to a gentle boil while stirring so it doesn't catch.
5. Simmer about 10 minutes until thickened, then stir in the cream and season with salt and pepper. (Optional: blitz smooth with a blender.)

**Pro tip:** swap the water for chicken stock for a deeper flavor, but keep total liquid under 300 ml per serving.

## To Drink: 奶茶 (Milk Tea)

- **Difficulty:** ★★
- **Time:** 30 minutes (almost all of it is just waiting — peak lazy brunch energy)

### Ingredients

- 袋泡红茶 (black tea bags, Lipton Yellow Label recommended) — 2 包 (约 4 克)
- 奶粉 (milk powder) — 11–12 克 (or use 淡奶/evaporated milk)
- 砂糖 (sugar) — 5–7 克

### Steps

1. Drop 2 tea bags into a cup and add 180–200 ml boiling water.
2. Wait 20–30 minutes. (Yes, really. Go lie down.)
3. Stir in the milk powder and sugar.
4. Mix well and serve.
